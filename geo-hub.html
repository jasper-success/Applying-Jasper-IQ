What is the GEO Hub? 🌐

The GEO Hub gives your marketing team a complete picture of how your brand appears in AI-generated search responses — across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude — and surfaces prioritized content recommendations to close visibility gaps.

📊Monitor brand visibility with a composite score and four supporting metrics
🎯Identify where competitors are cited and you're not
💡Surface content recommendations ranked by impact
⚡Push recommendations into Jasper Grid to generate fixes in one click
See it in action ▶
Setting Up Your First GEO Report
1
Add your site & select competitors Add up to 4 competitors most relevant to your goal and segment.
2
Select AI providers & queries Choose ChatGPT, Gemini, and/or Claude. Add topic clusters and queries — or use recommended ones.
3
Select pages Start with pages closest to the buying decision: product, pricing, case studies. Max 300 per report.
4
Choose your report frequency Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. Reports generate in ~10–15 minutes and refresh on your cadence.
Your 5 AI Visibility Metrics 📈
Overall AI Visibility Score Composite 0–100 blending brand presence, citation rate, and sentiment
Brand Presence Rate How often your brand appears in AI responses across tracked queries
Citation Rate How often AI platforms link to your content. Presence ≠ citation — only citations drive traffic.
Competitive Share of Voice Your visibility vs. tracked competitors across the same queries
Brand Sentiment Score How positively or negatively AI describes your brand when it mentions you
