Jasper IQ: Set Your Workspace Defaults 🎯

Make every generation smarter — automatically.

Why this matters 🎉

You've set up your brand foundation inside Jasper — Brand Voices, Audiences and a Knowledge Base. That puts you ahead of most teams. These are the building blocks that make Jasper generate content that reflects who you are, speaks to the right people and draws from your unique expertise.

These components don't work in isolation. When Brand Voice, Audience and Knowledge Base are all applied together, that's when Jasper generates content that sounds like you — consistently across every piece your whole team produces. Without all three, you're leaving competitive advantage on the table.

See it in action ▶
How to apply IQ to every generation
1
Go to Settings Navigate to Settings → Workspace Defaults in the top navigation bar.
2
Choose Your Defaults Select your Brand Voice, Audience, and Knowledge Base as your workspace defaults.
3
Hit Save — that's it! Your IQ settings are applied to every generation across your whole workspace automatically.
