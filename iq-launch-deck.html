Activating Jasper IQ — Workspace Defaults
[0:00] — update after filming
Two minutes.
That's all it takes.
1
Train your pillars Brand Voice, Audience, and Knowledge Base each take 5–10 min to set up — the resource hub walks you through each one
2
Go to Settings → Workspace Defaults Find it in the top navigation inside Jasper
3
Select your defaults Choose your Brand Voice, Audience, and Knowledge Base from the dropdowns
4
Hit Save — done Every generation across your whole workspace applies these settings automatically

Pro tip: Once Workspace Defaults are set, your team doesn't have to select anything manually. Every new piece of content — from any team member — comes out on-brand from the first draft.
